Personal

A few things that keep me going: running, art, and wild places.

Running

Running is my therapy, and I love being part of the running community. I have run two half marathons, and I was training for a full marathon until a knee injury forced me to stop. I am still hoping to make it to that 26.2-mile finish line one day.

Art

I think art gives life purpose and meaning, though I find it hard to explain why. When I look at a piece, I think about the process: what was going on in the artist's mind and life, how they felt about the experience they captured, and the love they poured into it. When I relate to the same experience, it clicks. Some of my favorite artists are Reid Christie, Will Rochfort, Momo Simic, Jonathan Houghton, D'lavigne, Richard Estes, and Seong Ryul, to name a few. Maybe one day I will be able to buy some of their original work.

Wild places and wolves

I love national parks. I am not an ecologist, and I am not sure I even know what that really entails, but I care deeply about the environment, animals, and people. Caring about environmental sustainability has been part of me since I was young, though I did not have the opportunity to study it where I grew up. Fate works in strange ways: my Ph.D. dissertation looked closely at sustainability, and that work now sits alongside this lifelong interest.

After a recent trip to Yellowstone, I became obsessed with Rick McIntyre's books about the wolves there, which led me to the Wolf Connection podcast, hosted by John Calfa, and the work of its team. I am constantly inspired by McIntyre, Calfa, Doug Smith, Marc Bekoff, Jane Goodall, and the many others who spend their lives understanding and protecting wild animals and the places they live.
